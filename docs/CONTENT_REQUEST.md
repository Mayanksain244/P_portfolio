## Part B: Real photos

### For the Works page (one cover photo per project)
- **Landscape**, the finished structure in daylight, with the road or bridge leading into the frame. An older photo from a phone is fine.
- At least **1600 px wide** if possible. Send the original file, not a WhatsApp-forwarded copy, because WhatsApp compresses images.
- Avoid photos that show people's faces up close unless they agreed, and photos with vehicle number plates in focus.

### For the Site Diary (gallery)
**These need no code from me.** Log in at `/admin` → Albums → New album → upload straight from a phone. Good album ideas:
- site inspections (one album per visit)
- monsoon / disaster-relief work (road clearing after landslides)
- before and after shots of a repaired stretch
- department events, trainings or awards

The upload shrinks and rotates photos automatically, so upload the originals.
